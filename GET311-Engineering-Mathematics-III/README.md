# GET 311: Engineering Mathematics III

**Credit:** 3 Units | **Status:** C (Compulsory) | **Contact:** LH 45

This folder holds a study companion for GET 311. For each topic in the course outline it gives:

- a **short introduction** to the topic, and
- **two application questions for each of the eight engineering departments** that take the course (16 questions per topic, 128 in total).

## Student Workbook

A print-ready student workbook is included as [GET311-Student-Workbook.pdf](GET311-Student-Workbook.pdf) and as an editable Word file, [GET311-Student-Workbook.docx](GET311-Student-Workbook.docx). It has a cover page, a progress tracker, a colour-coded section for each topic with its key ideas, all 128 questions with ruled space for working, and a review page at the end of each topic (83 A4 pages).

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course, students should be able to:

1. show a clear understanding of the course content, with broad knowledge of the areas covered;
2. have a deep enough foundation to go on to advanced mathematical topics;
3. develop simple algorithms and compute accurately;
4. write simple proofs of theorems and apply them; and
5. communicate mathematical knowledge well in speech, in writing and in groups.

## How to Use This Material

- Read the introduction to a topic before the lecture on it.
- Try the questions for your own department first, then do the others. Most of them use the same method in a different setting.
- The questions fit tutorials, group work (Learning Outcome 5) and small programming exercises in Python or MATLAB (Learning Outcome 3).
